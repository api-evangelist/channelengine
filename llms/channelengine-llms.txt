# ChannelEngine

> ChannelEngine is a marketplace integration and multichannel e-commerce platform (Leiden, Netherlands; backed by General Catalyst) that lets brands and retailers connect their ERP, PIM, WMS, or webstore once and sell across 1,300+ marketplaces and channels. It exposes three REST APIs — Merchant, Channel, and Channel Management — with API-key auth, JSON envelopes, page-based pagination, header-based rate limiting, and webhooks.

## APIs

- [Merchant API](https://support.channelengine.com/hc/en-us/sections/4406511272733-Merchant-API): Integrate your own systems (ERP/PIM/WMS/webstore); sync products, offers, orders, shipments, returns, cancellations, stock, bundles, webhooks.
- [Channel API](https://support.channelengine.com/hc/en-us/sections/4406503369629-Channel-API): Build your own marketplace on ChannelEngine; sync product/offer changes, create/invoice orders, handle shipments/returns/cancellations.
- [Channel Management API](https://support.channelengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409485647901-ChannelEngine-connect-to-the-Channel-Management-API): Export marketplace categories and product attributes to ChannelEngine.

## Auth

- API-key: pass the `apikey` query parameter (Settings > Merchant API keys). Base URL `https://{subdomain}.channelengine.net/api`.
- Platform SSO (web app only): OpenID Connect at https://login.channelengine.net/.well-known/openid-configuration

## SDKs

- First-party OpenAPI-generated clients (now deprecated; generate your own via OpenAPI Generator): PHP, C#, Python, Java, Ruby, Swift, JS — https://github.com/channelengine
- E-commerce connectors: Magento 2 / Adobe Commerce, WooCommerce.
